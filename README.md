# Hi, I'm Syeda Bushra Banu 👋

**Full Stack Developer** in Hyderabad, India — I build healthcare software end to end.

At **Today's Dental Services**, a multi-clinic dental group, I build the operations platform the staff
use every day and the websites patients see first.

### What I've built

- **Accounting & Bank Reconciliation** — a payment-matching engine that proves clinic payments reached
  the bank (grown from 8 to 13 passes; auto-match rates rose from ~30% to ~80% on real books), with
  Open Dental, Odoo ERP, Cherry and Authorize.Net integrations.
- **AI agents** — an on-site AI browser agent that collects settlement reports from financing portals,
  and an accounting AI assistant that guides staff through reconciliation and month-end close.
  Amounts and logins stay in plain code; passwords never reach the model.
- **Training & Certification** — a staff training portal, front end and AWS back end (API Gateway,
  Lambda, DynamoDB, S3, SES reminders), with anti-skip video tracking and e-signed PDF certificates.
- **Provider Credentialing** — replaced a spreadsheet with a 9-status workflow, live NPI checks,
  Excel import and expiry alerts (685 automated tests).
- **Online payments & security** — live Authorize.Net card payments for clinic bill-pay pages, plus a
  standalone secrets vault (encryption, per-organisation keys, role-based access, audit trail).
- **Healio** — a healthcare marketplace connecting patients with doctors, hospitals, labs, pharmacies
  and insurers: 17 of 33 NestJS modules, the React Native apps and a 52-page Next.js partner portal.
- **Clinic websites** — React, Astro and Vite sites with online booking, e-signed consent forms, an AI
  chat widget and JSON-LD SEO. [rockawayinternalmedicine.com](https://rockawayinternalmedicine.com)
  was built from scratch.
